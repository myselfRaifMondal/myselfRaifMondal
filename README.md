## What I work on

Markets are not one problem. They are a chain — information, research, signal, portfolio, execution, risk — and the chain feeds back on itself. Two questions pull me back repeatedly.

**How does distributed human intelligence become measurable?**
Good ideas are scattered across people and disciplines. I am interested in systems that can recognise useful insight without flattening what made it useful.

**How much edge survives execution?**
A model can be correct and still lose money. Latency, liquidity, market impact, inventory, and risk decide whether theoretical edge ever reaches the P&L.
