I build systems that turn market questions into measurable answers.

I came to quantitative finance from a tier-3 college, without the usual institutional access or network. A final-year internship at a quantitative trading firm changed how I read markets — less chart, more system: information, incentives, probability, execution. I have been building in that direction since.

## 🌸 創業 · IndiQuant

I am the founder of **[IndiQuant](https://indiquantresearch.in/)**, a quantitative research venture exploring how collective intelligence can produce a stronger understanding of Indian markets. Currently in private beta.

The public description stays high-level by design. The models, architecture, and strategy remain private.

## 🌸 研究 · What I work on

Markets are not one problem. They are a chain — information, research, signal, portfolio, execution, risk — and the chain feeds back on itself. Two questions pull me back repeatedly.

<p align="center">
  <code>information</code> → <code>research</code> → <code>signal</code> → <code>portfolio</code> → <code>execution</code> → <code>risk</code> ↺
</p>

<details open>
<summary><b>How does distributed human intelligence become measurable?</b></summary>
<br />

Good ideas are scattered across people and disciplines. I am interested in systems that can recognise useful insight without flattening what made it useful.

</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>How much edge survives execution?</b></summary>
<br />

A model can be correct and still lose money. Latency, liquidity, market impact, inventory, and risk decide whether theoretical edge ever reaches the P&L.

</details>
